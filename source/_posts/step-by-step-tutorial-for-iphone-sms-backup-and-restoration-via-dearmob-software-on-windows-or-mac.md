2\. Select **SMS** on the main interface, then you can view and choose the text messages that you want to back up.

3\. Click **Export** to export text messages from iPhone to your local PC.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can iPhone Text Messages Be Backed Up?

Yes, you can back up text messages on your iPhone with iCloud or sync messages with your Mac. If you are running a Windows PC, third-party iPhone backup software like DearMob iPhone Manager can help save text messages from iPhone to the computer.

### Can I Transfer My iPhone Text Messages to My Computer?

You can make a backup of all your iPhone data containing messages with iTunes. If you only want to transfer messages from iPhone to computer, you will need iPhone backup software. Open DearMob iPhone Manager and connect your iPhone. Go to SMS from the main interface. Then you can use the Export button to transfer any or all of the text messages you want to back up to your computer.
