## Encrypt Photos and Videos with Password on Mac

DearMob allows you to,  
**Password encrypt** files like photos, videos, contacts and iDevice backups.   
 Protect data with multiple **encryption algorithms**: 256-bit AES, 1024-bit RSA, PBKDF2, and Argon 2\. 

1\. Open DearMob iPhone Manager and plug your iPhone, iPad, or iPod into Mac.   
 2\. Click **Add files** to load photos and videos for encrypting.

![Add file for encryption on Mac](https://www.5kplayer.com/user-guide/images/userguide-how/guide-encrypt-add-mac.jpg) 

3\. Click **Encrypt Now** and the **Settings** window would pop up.  
 4\. Check the box "I've already know...", enter your new password, and then click OK.

![Set password for encryption](https://www.5kplayer.com/user-guide/images/userguide-how/guide-encrypt-password.jpg) 

![](https://www.5kplayer.com/user-guide/images/userguide-how/tips-icon.png)**Tips:**

* You can apply the same passwords to Photo, Video and Contact encryption.
* Create your password with numbers, uppercase letter and lowercase letter.
* The **Setting** window would pop out for you to set password for encryption, if you didn't set it before.

## Export Video, Photo, Contact with Password on Mac

1\. To encrypt photo during transferring, go to **Photo** interface > choose photos you for exporting to Mac > tick the box **Export with Encryption** \> and then press **Export**.

2\. Exporting video with encryption use a similar process as encrypting photos. Click into **Video** manager > select videos you for exporting > click **Export with Encryption** right beside **Select All** \> Export.

3\. To encrypt contacts, go to the **Contact** center > select contacts you want to export and encrypt > check **Export with Encryption** \> and click Export.
