 How do you avoid photos disappearing on iMessages?

 Always back up the data before updating or restoring the device. Another thing to make sure is enough space. Clearing the clutters on the iPhone will help it operate smoothly.

 It is frustrating not to see any photos sent to you, and there are many possibilities as to why it is happening. One cause might be the slow internet connection, while on the other hand, there is a software glitch.
