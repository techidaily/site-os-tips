## Export Files from iPhone/iPad Flash Drive to Computer

In DearMob, the files you've stored on your iOS device through the Flash Drive feature can be exported to your target computer within a few clicks. 

1\. Connect your iPhone to the computer with a USB cable or via Wi-Fi. 

 2\. On the Flash Drive interface, choose some or all of the stored file(s) or folder(s) you want to export to the computer. Click **Export**. Thus you've completed moving files from one computer to another computer through your iPhone/iPad as the go-between.

![Export Files from iPhone USB Drive to Mac](https://www.5kplayer.com/user-guide/images/userguide-how/export-files-usb-device-mac.jpg) 

![](https://www.5kplayer.com/user-guide/images/userguide-how/tips-icon.png)**Tips:**

* In this section, you can export files that were added to your iPhone via the Flash Drive feature in DearMob iPhone Manager.
* While for other files stored in iPhone storage, you can find them in other features in the main UI, such as Photo Transfer, Music Manager, Video & Video, Files, Podcast, Book, etc.

## Modify or Delete Files on iPhone/iPad Flash Drive

Through DearMob, the added files can be renamed or deleted. 

1\. Tick the file(s) you want to modify or delete.

 2\. Click **Modify** to edit file name or click **Delete.**

3\. Click **OK**.
