## Export Book on iPhone to PC

You can [remove books DRM](https://tools.techidaily.com/5kplayer/iphone-manager/) easily.

You can manage Epub, PDF, and audio books with DearMob iPhone Manager.

1\. To export ebooks, choose one book or some books.

2\. Click **Export** to transfer books from iPhone iPad to PC.

## Add New Book on iPhone

1\. Click **Add Book**, you can choose PDF, EPUB, Audiobook, M4B, and audio books to **Open**.

2\. Hit **Sync** to import the book to your iPhone.
