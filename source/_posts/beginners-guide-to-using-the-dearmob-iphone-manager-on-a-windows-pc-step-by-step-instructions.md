## Interface Introduction

The main interface of DearMob iPhone Manager is composed of fifteen modules based on different features. Efficient & intuitive.

![DearMob interface](https://www.5kplayer.com/user-guide/../user-guide/images/userguide-how/dearmob-interface.png) 

1\. **Photo Transfer**: Backup and transfer photos, manage album folder, etc.

2\. **Music Manager**: Manage song and playlists; make ringtone; edit music info, etc.

3\. **Video & Movie**: Transfer all kinds of videos across devices with format conversion option. 

4\. **Backup & Restore**: Back up and restore your iDevice with or without encryption.

5\. **Files**: Share files between devices; classify, modify, or delete files and file folders.

6\. **Data Security**: Encrypt & decrypt your local photo /video folders and other files.

7\. **Contact**: Export, import, add new, modify your contacts and groups.

8\. **Podcast**: Manage Podcast and iTunes U.

9\. **Book**: Transfer & convert eBooks.

10\. **Ringtone**: Manage Ringtone and Voice Memos.

11\. **App**: Install & uninstall apps, manage app files.

12\. **SMS**: Back up your messages to the local computer and save media from within.

13\. **Calendar**: Backup, add, import calendar.

14\. **Bookmark**: Transfer, add, edit Bookmarks.

15\. **Flash Drive**: Mount your iPhone as a thumb drive.
