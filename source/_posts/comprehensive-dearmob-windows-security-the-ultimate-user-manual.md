## Export Files with Encryption

1\. To export photo with encryption, click on **Photo** \> select the photos you want to export with encryption > click **Export with Encryption** \> **Export**.

2\. The steps to export video with encryption: In **Video** interface > select the video you want to export with encryption > click **Export with Encryption** \> Export.

3\. In **Contact** interface > select the contact info you want to export with encryption > click **Export with Encryption** \> Export.
