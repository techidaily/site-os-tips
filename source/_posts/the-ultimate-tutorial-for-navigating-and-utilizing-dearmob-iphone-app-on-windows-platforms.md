2\. **Music Manager**: Manage song and playlists; make ringtone; edit music info, etc.

3\. **Video & Movie**: Transfer all kinds of videos across devices with format conversion option. 

4\. **Backup & Restore**: Back up and restore your iDevice with or without encryption.

5\. **Files**: Share files between devices; classify, modify, or delete files and file folders.

15\. **Flash Drive**: Mount your iPhone as a thumb drive.
